# Outreach kit

Backlinks from Missouri news sites and political blogs are the fastest way for a
new domain to rank. Pitch the **link-worthy assets**, not the site as a whole:
editors link to a source they can cite, not to a hit piece.

## What to pitch

| Asset | URL | Why someone would link to it |
|---|---|---|
| The Josh Hawley timeline | https://joshhawleygop.com/timeline | Every key vote and reversal from 2016 to today, in order, each with a source. A reference reporters and bloggers can drop into a story. |
| Josh Hawley by the numbers | https://joshhawleygop.com/by-the-numbers | 15 citeable stats (51–50, $1.7M, 10 months, 0 cosponsors), each linked to its source. Built to be quoted. |
| Stalled Medicaid bill post | https://joshhawleygop.com/news/hawley-medicaid-repeal-bill-stalled | A news hook: 14 months, zero cosponsors, no action, straight from the official bill status data. |
| Amendment 3 explainer | https://joshhawleygop.com/news/amendment-3-2026-ballot-hawley | Timely until November 3, 2026. |

## Who to contact

Verify each outlet's current tips and op-ed contacts on its site before
sending; don't mass-mail. One personalized note to the right reporter or
editor beats twenty cold emails. Several of these block automated checks, so
confirm each is still publishing.

### Statewide and public media

- **Missouri Independent** (missouriindependent.com): nonprofit statehouse and federal delegation coverage. Already cited throughout the site.
- **KCUR 89.3** (kcur.org): Kansas City NPR station; covers Hawley and Amendment 3 closely.
- **St. Louis Public Radio** (stlpr.org): same for St. Louis.
- **KBIA** (kbia.org) and **KOMU** (komu.com): Columbia and mid-Missouri.
- **Missourinet** (missourinet.com): statewide radio network news.
- **The Missouri Times** (themissouritimes.com): statehouse politics trade publication.

### Newspapers (op-eds and letters to the editor are the realistic way in)

- **Kansas City Star** (kansascity.com): its editorial board has written about Hawley's residency and January 6 at length.
- **St. Louis Post-Dispatch** (stltoday.com)
- **Springfield News-Leader** (news-leader.com): Hawley's Missouri properties are in Christian County, next to Springfield. The residency story is local here.
- **Columbia Missourian** (columbiamissourian.com): wrote an editorial on his residency in 2020.
- **Hannibal Courier-Post**: also editorialized on his residency.

### Independent and alternative outlets

- **The Beacon** (thebeaconnews.org): Kansas City nonprofit news.
- **The Pitch** (thepitchkc.com): Kansas City alt-weekly.
- **Riverfront Times** (riverfronttimes.com): St. Louis alt-weekly. Check it's still active.
- **Show Me Progress** (showmeprogress.com): progressive Missouri politics blog. Check recent activity first.

### Organizations that may link to a resource

- **Stop the Ban Missouri**: the coalition opposing the 2026 Amendment 3. The Amendment 3 explainer and the abortion topic page are directly relevant.
- Missouri veterans' groups, for the PACT Act page.
- Rural hospital advocates, for the Medicaid page and the stalled-bill post.

### Community forums

r/missouri, r/kansascity, r/StLouis and r/springfieldMO. Read each
subreddit's self-promotion rules first. Share a specific page in a relevant
thread where it answers a question; don't drop the homepage. Reddit links
are mostly nofollow, but they drive real traffic, which the stats dashboard
will show under "Top referrers".

## Pitch email template

Keep it short, lead with the useful thing, and disclose what the site is.

> **Subject:** Sourced Josh Hawley timeline, free to cite
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> I read your [piece on X / coverage of Amendment 3]. I run The Hawley Record
> (joshhawleygop.com), an independent site tracking Sen. Josh Hawley's record.
> It's opinionated and I'm upfront about that, but every factual claim links
> to its source, mostly outlets like yours.
>
> Two pages you might find useful:
>
> - A timeline of his key votes and reversals since 2016, each entry sourced:
>   https://joshhawleygop.com/timeline
> - One detail I haven't seen reported: the bill Hawley filed on July 15,
>   2025 to undo the Medicaid cuts he'd voted for two weeks earlier has zero
>   cosponsors and no action since it was referred to committee, per the
>   official bill status data: https://joshhawleygop.com/news/hawley-medicaid-repeal-bill-stalled
>
> Feel free to use or link either. If you spot anything wrong, tell me and I'll
> fix it.
>
> [Your name]
> [City], Missouri

For a letter to the editor or op-ed, lead with one argument (the residency
story works best for the Springfield News-Leader; Medicaid for rural papers),
stay under the outlet's word limit (usually 200–250 words for letters), and
include one link at most.

## Don't

- Don't buy links, join link exchanges, or post in comment sections. Google
  penalizes it and it discredits the site.
- Don't pitch the site as neutral. Say it's independent and sourced, which is true.
