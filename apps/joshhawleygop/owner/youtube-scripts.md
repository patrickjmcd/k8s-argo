# YouTube scripts

Three production-ready scripts for the strongest topics, written for YouTube
search: the keyword leads the title, the description's first two lines carry
the hook and the link, and chapters give Google "key moments". Each narration
is roughly 650–750 words, which runs about 4–5 minutes at a normal pace.

## Before you publish: footage rights

- **Safe to use:** footage released by the House January 6 committee, and
  C-SPAN floor video under C-SPAN's non-commercial use terms. Senate floor
  video is public record. Screenshots of headlines you're discussing are
  generally fair use in commentary; keep them brief and talk over them.
- **Needs a license:** the famous fist-pump photo was taken by an E&E News
  (Politico) photographer, and Politico objected when Hawley's campaign used
  it. License it, or describe it over a public-domain shot of the Capitol.
- Use your own voice or a clearly labeled text-to-speech voice. Don't clone
  anyone's voice, and don't generate fake footage of Hawley.
- Pin a comment with the sources link, and add the site in the channel's
  "Links" section.
