## 2016–2018: Attorney general, then Senate candidate

### 2016: The "Ladders" ad

Running for Missouri attorney general, Hawley stars in a TV ad attacking "career politicians just climbing the ladder, using one office to get another." ([University News](https://unewsonline.com/2018/10/actions-speak-louder-than-words-josh-hawleys-political-climb/))

### January 2017: Sworn in as attorney general

Political consultants who later run his Senate campaign begin directing taxpayer-paid staff in the AG's office the same month, a state audit later finds. ([St. Louis Post-Dispatch](https://www.stltoday.com/news/local/government-politics/hawleys-political-consultants-coordinated-with-his-taxpayer-paid-staff-audit-says/article_77c9d53e-a351-5cdc-847e-55025eb85987.html))

### October 10, 2017: Announces Senate run

Ten months into his first elected office. ([Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2017/10/10/hawley-announces-bid-against-mccaskill/)) Details: [the ladder climber](/ladder-climber).

### November 2018: Elected to the U.S. Senate

Defeats Sen. Claire McCaskill. ([PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/republican-josh-hawley-wins-missouri-senate-race)) In December, he's under investigation over his conduct as AG. ([KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/government/2018-12-10/under-investigation-sen-elect-josh-hawley-for-his-conduct-as-missouri-attorney-general)) Republican Secretary of State Jay Ashcroft closes the case in 2019, finding no evidence he broke election law.

## 2019–2020: The move to Virginia

### January 2019: Buys a house in Vienna, Virginia

Weeks after being sworn in; now valued at around $1.7 million. That spring he sells his Columbia, Missouri home and registers to vote at his sister's house in Ozark. ([Kansas City Star via AOL](https://www.aol.com/sen-josh-hawley-once-faced-175323582.html))

### 2020: The Kansas City Star reports on his residency

Missouri editorial boards pile on. ([Columbia Missourian](https://www.columbiamissourian.com/opinion/guest_commentaries/editorial-sen-josh-hawley-pretends-his-lack-of-a-missouri-address-is-an-elitist-attack/article_9d48efa4-2e9c-11eb-aacc-575c134ccefe.html)) Details: [where Josh Hawley lives](/missouri-residency).

## 2021–2022: January 6 and after

### January 6, 2021: The fist pump, the objection, the run

Raises a fist to the crowd outside the Capitol, objects to Pennsylvania's electoral votes (rejected 92–7), and is later filmed fleeing the mob. ([Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Counting_of_electoral_votes_(January_6-7,_2021)), [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/josh-hawley-seen-fleeing-trump-mob-riled-newly-released-jan-6-footage-rcna39490)) Details: [January 6](/january-6).

### January 2021: "Blood on his hands"

The Kansas City Star editorial board blames him for the violence; Simon & Schuster drops his book deal, and he calls it a First Amendment violation. ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/news/533071-hawley-hometown-paper-says-he-has-blood-on-his-hands/), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2021/01/08/955103447/gop-leaders-condemn-sen-josh-hawley-after-pro-trump-riot-at-u-s-capitol))

### 2021: A Christian County, Missouri house is finished

Built on land his parents bought, with Hawley on the deed. ([Kansas City Star via AOL](https://www.aol.com/sen-josh-hawley-once-faced-175323582.html))

### March 2022: The Jackson smear, and the mugs

Claims Judge Ketanji Brown Jackson went soft on child pornography offenders; PolitiFact finds he cherry-picked. His campaign refuses to stop selling Jan. 6 fist-pump mugs. ([PolitiFact](https://politifact.com/factchecks/2022/mar/24/josh-hawley/josh-hawley-misleads-judge-jackson-and-child-porno/), [KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/politics-elections-and-government/2022-03-21/josh-hawley-refuses-to-stop-selling-merchandise-featuring-his-pre-riot-jan-6-fist-pump))

### June–August 2022: The PACT Act flip-flop

Yes on burn-pit care in June, no in July, yes again in August after veterans protest. ([Missouri Independent](https://missouriindependent.com/2022/07/28/these-people-dont-care-u-s-senate-gop-stalls-bill-for-veterans-exposed-to-burn-pits/), [Missouri Independent](https://missouriindependent.com/2022/08/02/u-s-senate-in-turnaround-backs-aid-for-veterans-exposed-to-burn-pits/)) Details: [the PACT Act](/pact-act-veterans).

### July 2022: The running video airs

The January 6 committee plays footage of Hawley fleeing; the Kansas City Star calls him a "laughingstock." ([Common Dreams](https://www.commondreams.org/news/2022/07/24/major-missouri-newspaper-calls-josh-hawley-laughingstock-fleeing-jan-6-mob))

## 2023–2024: Re-election

### May 2023: Publishes *Manhood*

([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhood:_The_Masculine_Virtues_America_Needs))

### Late 2023: Buys a second Christian County house

Heading into his re-election year. ([Kansas City Star via AOL](https://www.aol.com/sen-josh-hawley-once-faced-175323582.html))

### October 2024: The "stolen valor" attack

Accuses Marine veteran Lucas Kunce of "stolen valor"; Border Patrol confirms Kunce's training. ([Kunce campaign, partisan source](https://lucaskunce.com/news/2024/10/17/missouri-veterans-bipartisan-veteran-leaders-and-veteran-organizations-condemn-hawley-for-disgraceful-defamation/))

### November 5, 2024: Re-elected; Missouri passes Amendment 3

Hawley wins 55.6% against Kunce. The same day, voters put abortion rights in the state constitution with 51.7%. ([Missouri Independent](https://missouriindependent.com/2024/11/05/josh-hawley-lucas-kunce-missouri-senate/), [Missouri Independent](https://missouriindependent.com/2024/11/05/missouri-voters-overturn-states-near-total-abortion-ban/))

## 2025–2026: Medicaid and the abortion rematch

### February 2025: One vote for burn-pit veterans

The only Republican to back a resolution protecting burn-pit veterans' benefits. ([Task & Purpose](https://taskandpurpose.com/news/senate-vote-pact-act/))

### May 2025: "We must ignore calls to cut Medicaid"

His New York Times op-ed calls cuts "morally wrong." ([MSNBC](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/gops-josh-hawley-pushes-undo-medicaid-cuts-just-voted-rcna219053))

### July 1, 2025: Votes for the Medicaid cuts

The One Big Beautiful Bill passes the Senate 51–50. ([KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/health/2025-07-01/big-beautiful-bill-medicaid-cuts-hawley-schmitt-missouri)) Details: [the Medicaid vote](/medicaid-vote).

### July 15, 2025: Files a bill to undo some of them

S. 2279 is referred to the Finance Committee and never moves. ([GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/bulkdata/BILLSTATUS/119/s/BILLSTATUS-119s2279.xml))

### February 2026: "I absolutely am going to be involved"

Commits to campaigning for the 2026 Amendment 3 abortion ban. ([KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/politics-elections-and-government/2026-02-22/hawley-and-schmitt-believe-voters-will-restrict-abortion-and-back-a-new-congressional-map))

### September 2026: The "Declaration for Life"

Launches a national push to ban mifepristone; the same week, rules out a 2028 presidential run. ([Hawley's Senate office](https://www.hawley.senate.gov/hawley-unveils-declaration-for-life-with-christian-leaders/), [Political Wire](https://politicalwire.com/2026/09/17/josh-hawley-rules-out-2028-run-for-president/))

### September 29, 2026: The Jack Smith mix-up

After Jack Smith's Senate testimony, Hawley tells Fox News that Smith went through Melania Trump's "personal drawers," in a search that happened three months before Smith was appointed. ([HuffPost](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/josh-hawleys-jack-smith-error-on-fox-news_n_6abcd224e4b0ecc03a1da87e)) Details: [the Fox News error, explained](/news/hawley-jack-smith-melania-fox-news-error).
