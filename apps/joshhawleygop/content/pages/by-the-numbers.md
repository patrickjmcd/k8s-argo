## Where he lives

### $1.7 million

Estimated value of the five-bedroom house in Vienna, Virginia that Hawley bought in January 2019, weeks after being sworn in. ([Kansas City Star via AOL](https://www.aol.com/sen-josh-hawley-once-faced-175323582.html)) [The Virginia house story](/missouri-residency).

## Medicaid

### 51–50

The Senate vote on the One Big Beautiful Bill, with the Vice President breaking the tie. Hawley voted yes; his no would have stopped it. ([KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/health/2025-07-01/big-beautiful-bill-medicaid-cuts-hawley-schmitt-missouri)) [The Medicaid vote timeline](/medicaid-vote).

### About 1.25 million

Missourians covered by Medicaid and CHIP. ([Healthinsurance.org](https://www.healthinsurance.org/medicaid/missouri/), [Missouri Foundation for Health](https://mffh.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/2025-MO-Medicaid-Basics-fact-sheet.pdf))

### 14 days

Time between voting for the Medicaid cuts (July 1, 2025) and filing a bill to undo some of them (July 15, 2025). ([MSNBC](https://www.ms.now/rachel-maddow-show/maddowblog/gops-josh-hawley-pushes-undo-medicaid-cuts-just-voted-rcna219053))

### 0

Cosponsors on that bill, S. 2279, which hasn't moved since it was referred to committee. ([GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/bulkdata/BILLSTATUS/119/s/BILLSTATUS-119s2279.xml))

### $50 billion

The rural hospital fund Hawley says he got in exchange for his vote. ([KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/health/2025-07-01/big-beautiful-bill-medicaid-cuts-hawley-schmitt-missouri))

## Abortion and elections

### 51.7%

Share of Missourians who voted for Amendment 3 in 2024, putting abortion rights in the state constitution. Hawley opposed it. ([Missouri Independent](https://missouriindependent.com/2024/11/05/missouri-voters-overturn-states-near-total-abortion-ban/)) [The Amendment 3 fight](/abortion-amendment-3).

### 47%

Support for the 2026 Amendment 3 abortion ban in a Saint Louis University/YouGov poll, short of a majority, with 40% opposed and 12% undecided. ([KCUR](https://www.kcur.org/politics-elections-and-government/2026-05-27/missouri-amendment-3-abortion-stop-the-ban))

### 55.6%

Hawley's share of the vote in his 2024 re-election. ([Missouri Independent](https://missouriindependent.com/2024/11/05/josh-hawley-lucas-kunce-missouri-senate/))

The next time Josh Hawley's name is on a Missouri ballot. ([FAQ](/faq))
