Former special counsel Jack Smith testified before the Senate Judiciary Committee on September 29, 2026, defending his two investigations of Donald Trump. Afterward, Josh Hawley went on Fox News with Sean Hannity to explain why Smith was "totally out of control."

## What Hawley said

> "He went through Melania Trump's personal drawers, clothing, items very personal to her. I mean, this is ridiculous, just ridiculous."

## What actually happened

The search Hawley was describing is the FBI's search of Mar-a-Lago on **August 8, 2022**, in the classified documents investigation. Attorney General Merrick Garland appointed Jack Smith as special counsel on **November 18, 2022**, more than three months later.

Smith couldn't have gone through anyone's drawers in that search. He didn't have the job yet. HuffPost reported no correction from Hawley.

## A Missouri double feature

Hawley wasn't the only Missouri senator to fumble a "gotcha" that day. At the hearing itself, Sen. Eric Schmitt accused Smith of perjury over what he said was an Atlanta Hawks NBA game. The game in question was actually an Iowa Hawkeyes college game.

### In fairness

Conservative outlets praised Hawley's questioning of Smith at the hearing itself. The error came afterward, on Fox News.
{: .fair }
