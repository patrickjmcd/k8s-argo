On September 17, 2026, Josh Hawley unveiled a "Declaration for Life" with a group of Christian leaders. The document calls on Congress to ban mifepristone, the drug used in medication abortion, which accounted for 63% of U.S. abortions in 2023, and urges lawmakers to sign on.

## What Hawley wants

"It's time to take a vote on my legislation to ban the chemical abortion drug mifepristone," Hawley said. He framed the declaration as a call to "everyday Christians across America," and he's pushing Republican candidates to sign it.

## Why it matters in Missouri

Missouri voters put abortion rights in the state constitution in 2024. A federal ban on mifepristone would override that for the most common method of abortion, no matter how Missourians vote on [the 2026 Amendment 3](/news/amendment-3-2026-ballot-hawley). It's the same senator who says he's fighting for Missouri, asking Washington to overrule it.

## Also this month

The same week, Hawley told the Washington Examiner he has "no intention" of running for president in 2028. His term in the Senate runs until January 2031.
